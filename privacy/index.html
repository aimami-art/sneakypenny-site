Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Sneaky Penny: 3D Puzzle (“the game”) on iOS or Android. The game is published by Finamap Yazılım ve Dan. Ltd. Şti. (“we”, “us”).

In short: the game needs no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising identifier; where the law requires it, you are asked first, and you can say no.

1. What is saved on your device

The game saves the following on your own device so that you can continue where you left off:

  • your progress (the levels you have reached);
  • your coins, hearts and boosters, and what you have bought in the game;
  • your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and language.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the game or clear its data. It may be included in your device’s own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), which are controlled by you and by Apple or Google.

2. What we do not collect

We ourselves do not collect, store or sell personal information about you through the game; the advertising described in section 4 is handled by Google. The game has no sign-up, no chat and no social features, and it does not read your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

3. Purchases

Purchases made in the game are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card number or other payment details. The store only tells the game that a purchase was completed, so that the game can deliver it.

4. Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels from level 6 on, and, only when you choose to watch one, a rewarded ad that gives you a booster or a heart. Players who own Premium see no ads between levels.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android, the IDFA on iOS), its IP address, its model and settings, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Your choice. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS, the game asks for permission before your device’s advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Changing your mind. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

The game contains no other advertising or analytics tools.

5. Other companies involved

  • Apple and Google distribute the game. Depending on your device settings, they may share with us anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of downloads and crash reports. These do not identify you.
  • Google provides the ads in the game (Google AdMob), as described in section 4.
  • Unity is the technology the game is built with. When the game runs, Unity may receive basic technical information about the device, such as its model and operating-system version. See Unity’s Privacy Policy.

6. When you contact us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. We do not share it with others, and we delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to.

7. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since the game itself sends us nothing, this concerns only the messages you send us; for advertising data, see Google’s Privacy Policy linked in section 4. To use these rights, write to the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
